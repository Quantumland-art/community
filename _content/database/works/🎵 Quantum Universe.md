### Synopsis

**Quantum Universe** is a symphony for organ, [[💾 Quantum Guitar]] and electronics. Its world premiere took place on 20 November 2025 at Merton College Chapel as the opening concert of the [[📍 Oxford Quantum Centenary Conference Concerts]].

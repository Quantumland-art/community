[Oxford Quantum Centenary Conference: What Quantum?](https://www.philosophy.ox.ac.uk/event/oxford-quantum-centenary-conference-what-quantum)

Concert events connected to the Oxford Quantum Centenary Conference, hosted by the Faculty of Philosophy at the University of Oxford as part of the 2025 International Year of Quantum Science and Technology.

### Opening concert

World premiere of [[🎵 Quantum Universe]], a symphony for organ, [[💾 Quantum Guitar]] and electronics.

- Date: 20 November 2025
- Venue: Merton College Chapel
- Composer and organ: [[👤 Rakhat-Bi Abdyssagin]]
- Quantum Guitar: [[👤 Bob Coecke]]
- Video: [Rakhat-Bi Abdyssagin 'Quantum Universe' Symphony for Organ, Quantum Guitar and Electronics (2025)](https://youtu.be/1Dl8BGqTuE0)

### Closing concert

Industrial electronics and classical piano concert at Holywell Music Room.

- Date: 23 November 2025
- Venue: Holywell Music Room
- Performers: [[👤 Rakhat-Bi Abdyssagin]], [[👤 Bob Coecke]] and [[👤 Bruce Warner]]
- Video: [Industrial and Classical Music Concert by Rakhat-Bi Abdyssagin, Bob Coecke and Bruce Warner](https://youtu.be/jlo-HaAfyrY)

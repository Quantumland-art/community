### About

Quantum Guitar is a quantum musical instrument that augments an electric guitar by coupling it with Moth's Actias quantum synth. The instrument maps playable guitar states to qubit states and lets the performer manipulate qubit rotations and measurements using foot controllers, while retaining a continuous transition between classical guitar sound and quantum-enhanced sound.
